# Real physics
> Liquids, ice, and hands move like real life. Nothing floats, morphs, or hovers.

## Checklist
- Pours are narrow natural streams that land, settle, and stop when the pour stops
- Ice shifts and crackles when something is poured over it
- Condensation drips down, never up
- Hands move at real everyday speed, one simple action per clip
- Anatomically correct hands, exactly five fingers, no hovering or posing

## For the prompt writer
Describe how the product physically behaves in the PRODUCT PHYSICS block and
hold the model to it. Anti-AI rule: "Real physics: liquids move only while
poured and settle when it stops."
