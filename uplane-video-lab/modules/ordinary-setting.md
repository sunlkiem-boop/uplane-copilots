# Real, everyday locations
> A normal kitchen or car someone actually lives in. Not a set, not a showroom.

## Checklist
- Ordinary and lived-in: normal light, a few real everyday objects softly out of focus
- Nothing styled: no candles, no set dressing, no showroom minimalism
- No brands or logos anywhere except the product itself
- Fridges: plain groceries only, never other branded products

## For the prompt writer
Write this into the SETTING block. Call out fridge interiors explicitly (a
known failure point that invents brands). Anti-AI rule: "The settings are
ordinary and lived-in, not styled or showroom."
