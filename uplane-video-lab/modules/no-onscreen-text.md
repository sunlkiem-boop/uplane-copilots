# No on-screen text
> The video comes out with zero text. Captions, logo, and promo get added in editing.

## Checklist
- No captions, subtitles, stickers, or watermarks in the generation
- The only readable text anywhere is the product's own label
- Captions in editing: use the script lines word for word, one per clip
- Logo and promo chip in editing: small and clean, near the end

## For the prompt writer
Ban text in FORMAT AND CAMERA and repeat it in the anti-AI rules. Put
everything text-related in the POST notes: captions verbatim to the voiceover
lines, the brand logo, the promo chip.
