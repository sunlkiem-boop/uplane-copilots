# No faces
> Nobody's face appears anywhere. Not even in reflections.

## Checklist
- Frame on hands, forearms, the counter, or the product only
- No head, no chin, no visible speaker at any point
- No face in reflections: fridge door, car windows, rear-view mirror, glasses, glossy surfaces
- Any face moment in the original gets a face-free replacement that does the same job

## For the prompt writer
State it in FORMAT AND CAMERA and again in the anti-AI rules: "No face, ever,
in any clip, including reflections. Hands, forearms, and product only." When a
source beat features a face, replace it with a face-free beat that keeps the
same function (e.g. a walking clip becomes the product sliding into a car cup
holder, forearm only).
