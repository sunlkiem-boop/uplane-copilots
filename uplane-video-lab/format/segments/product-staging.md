— MATCH THE REFERENCE: the attached product reference image(s) are the single source of truth — match shape, proportions, finish, label layout, logo, badges, colours, and every word of label text EXACTLY. Invent nothing the references do not show.
— EXACTLY ONE of each product unit unless a beat explicitly calls for more — no duplicate, second, mirrored, or background copy of the product; no warped or doubled packaging.
— REFERENCED FACES ONLY: show only product faces that appear in an attached reference image. The FRONT (front reference) is the default hero face; the BACK, a side, or any other face may appear ONLY when its own reference image is attached — and must then match that reference exactly. A face with no attached reference never appears (it renders as garbled AI text).
— NO FINE-PRINT MACROS: even for a referenced face, keep the product at a comfortable medium distance. Dense small print (ingredients panels, supplement facts) garbles when rendered large — never an extreme close-up macro of fine print. A "label proof" or read-the-label moment stays a medium-distance hero shot of the referenced face; anything the viewer must actually read rides on large label text or a caption added in post.
— BRAND CONTAINMENT: the client's brand name, logo, label design, and signature accent colour appear ONLY on the client product that matches the attached reference. NEVER on a stand-in, a competitor product, background packaging, a phone screen, signage, a wall, or any other surface. Every generic or competitor prop stays completely anonymous: blank, no logo, no readable text, not tinted the brand colour.
— PRODUCT-SPECIFIC HAZARDS: the PHYSICAL PRODUCT SPEC above defines THIS product's own staging hazards — hidden countable arrangements, multi-component kit staging, proportion locks. Obey it exactly; it outranks any beat that conflicts with it.
— If the reviewed beats ever conflict with these rules, these rules win.
